# Syfe

> Syfe is a Singapore-headquartered digital wealth management platform, licensed by the Monetary
> Authority of Singapore under Capital Markets Services licence CMS100837 and operating in
> Singapore, Hong Kong and Australia (as Selfwealth by Syfe). It offers robo-advised managed
> portfolios, a self-directed brokerage, and Cash+ cash management to retail, private-wealth and
> business clients. **Syfe publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, API reference,
> OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, SDK, CLI, webhook catalogue or MCP server. An agent cannot
> integrate with Syfe programmatically; the only public surface is the marketing site, the help
> centre and the mobile/web applications.

## Products

- [Managed Portfolios](https://www.syfe.com/managed-portfolio): Robo-advised ETF portfolios.
- [Core](https://www.syfe.com/core): Core Defensive, Core Balanced, Core Growth, Equity100.
- [REIT+](https://www.syfe.com/reit-plus): Singapore REIT portfolio.
- [Income+](https://www.syfe.com/income-plus): Income-oriented portfolios.
- [Select Themes](https://www.syfe.com/select-themes): Thematic portfolios (ESG & clean energy, disruptive technology, healthcare innovation, China growth).
- [Brokerage](https://www.syfe.com/brokerage): Self-directed trading in US, Singapore, Hong Kong and London listed stocks, ETFs and options.
- [Cash Management](https://www.syfe.com/cash-management): Cash+ Flexi and Cash+ Guaranteed.
- [Private Wealth](https://www.syfe.com/private-wealth): High-net-worth advisory.
- [Syfe for Business](https://www.syfe.com/syfe-for-business): Corporate treasury cash management. Advisor-led onboarding, no partner API.
